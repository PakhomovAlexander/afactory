Resource management architecture decisions
===========================================

Proposed decisions for eventual ADRs. Numbers are assigned only on integration into AF.
User-approved policy: cooperative default; strict requirements never silently downgraded.

A. One resource domain with durable coordination and no mandatory daemon
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Use a local transactional domain store shared by participating coordinators. Keep all model
budgets, Task authority and business results in existing Task journals. Durable intents and
receipts connect the stores; a resource reservation is not a Task verdict.

Rejected: an in-process semaphore (independent AF commands overcommit); a mandatory machine
daemon (conflicts with AF's operating model and installation simplicity); network-shared
SQLite for a cluster (does not supply a validated distributed authority/locking contract).

Consequence: active commands advance the queue. Autonomous resumption after every coordinator
exits requires an explicitly operated runner/service. Cluster node allocation stays with
Kubernetes. Domain setup has to identify overlapping allocations and incompatible engines.

B. Full memory reservations and non-preemptive fair admission
------------------------------------------------------------

Reserve each admitted execution's whole memory ceiling. Use weighted reservation-occupancy
service and an age-triggered drain turn. Nested Tasks remain in their root's fair-share account.

Rejected: admit against instantaneous RSS (running workers can grow together); reserve only
historical peaks with no ceiling (measurements are not worst-case bounds); strict FIFO alone
(unnecessary blocking); unlimited small-job backfill (large jobs starve); arbitrary suspension
or eviction (memory is not freed by pause, provider state/effects may be lost).

Consequence: utilization may be lower than an overcommitting scheduler. AF exposes the cost of
unused reservations and suggests future plan changes. Finite operation bounds and stable
capacity are prerequisites for progress claims; there is no unconditional start-time SLA.

C. Capability-specific enforcement with a cooperative default
-------------------------------------------------------------

One contract reports hard, cooperative, observed and unavailable capabilities by resource and
scope. Ordinary local work may use cooperative scheduling. Strict work requires a capable
boundary. Resource policy never weakens security isolation or changes execution destination.

Rejected: require delegated cgroups on all machines (excludes common desktops and containers);
claim sampling is hard isolation (false); silently remove strict requirements (changes the
approved contract); automatically launch containers/Jobs (introduces unapproved effects).

Consequence: native Linux, containers and native desktops can share interfaces without making
identical protection claims. Each backend needs its own capability probes and qualification.

D. Recover occupied resources by execution identity
--------------------------------------------------

Owner death moves an unresolved reservation to reconciliation. released_proven requires the
owned boundary empty or definitively terminal with its launch gate sealed. Ordinary cooperative
work may use retired_cooperative only under design.txt section 9: verified tracked identities
terminal, launcher/root terminal, sealed gate and a durable residual-risk receipt. Unknown root,
launcher or tracked survivor stays quarantined. Neither retirement nor partial observation proves
whole-tree cleanup. Persist exact IDs and the Task-authorized permit before effectful work.

Rejected: free on PID absence, flock release or timeout alone (descendants/remote work can
survive); retry a remote create under a new name after losing its response (duplicate work).

Consequence: an unavailable backend can leave a visible quarantined reservation. That is a
truthful uncertainty, with an inspection action. Reconciliation never fabricates provider usage
or promises exactly-once behavior from an external service.

E. Explainable decisions with bounded telemetry
-----------------------------------------------

Every admission/wait/refusal has structured constraints, quantities, origins and evidence.
Keep decision receipts and final resource summaries durable; keep detailed samples bounded.
The CLI, TUI and agents consume the same read model.

Rejected: a single opaque health score (cannot guide a human); unbounded raw metrics (resource
management becomes the bottleneck); inferring root causes from log text or high utilization
alone (confuses evidence and inference); treating status projections as execution authority.

Consequence: explanations expose uncertainty, simultaneous blockers and stale samples. Human
history remains useful after raw metrics expire, and active evidence remains protected by the
existing Storage Budget/reachability contract.

F. Durable policy identity, one-use authorization and storage growth
------------------------------------------------------------------

A persistent domain UUID survives boot/allocation epochs; registered Task Stores and execution
identities remain reachable across replacement. Fence the previous store writer before binding
a new epoch; reconcile remote work independently. A new pod UID is not termination evidence.
Task-journal commit of a one-use permit is the authorization point. It follows domain claim
arming and validates the current Task writer in that same Task transaction. A committed permit
survives writer handoff as already-authorized in-flight work. Takeover closes unconsumed claims
or reconciles consumed launchers; it never treats a paused launcher as an empty execution.

Disk is a vector resource per actual backing volume: current free blocks less the existing
storage floor, unallocated emergency shortfall and outstanding incremental growth. Allocation
transfers a growth hold into filesystem occupancy once; retained output transfers ownership
before scratch retirement. tmpfs consumes the total memory ceiling. Disk scratch must be an
explicit executor capability instead of silently inheriting a tmpfs convention.

Recovery slots are physically allocated before admission, synchronized before reporting a
receipt durable, then replayed into Task journals. Strict recovery-space claims require qualified
protected backing. Unrecoverable storage failure leaves unknown evidence and charged intents;
64 MiB of bookkeeping or a sparse file is not a durability guarantee.

These decisions amend ADR-0144 storage admission/scratch conventions and ADR-0154 sampled
cooperative overage behavior in addition to the small-host ADR-0148 family. Existing strict
profiles retain their contract until an explicit reviewed migration. Cooperative overshoot is
visible extra occupancy and a warning, not invented hard enforcement or an inferred OOM.

G. Review D closures: authority, liveness and operability
-------------------------------------------------------

Canonical OS-account/allocation rendezvous is independent of HOME/XDG; unavailable discovery
never silently creates a second aggregate budget. Host identity is distinct from boot identity;
cross-host successor cannot declare predecessor-local execution dead. Remote authorization is
published only after the unique domain claim is consumed, and executor sealing prevents delayed
publication from bypassing recovery. Pod claims deduplicate only already-authorized execution.

Known nested maxima are reserved with parents. Unplanned live-parent expansion is immediate-or-
conflict, never queued holding capacity. One domain-wide drain turn uses a defined vector predicate,
excludes unbounded holds, and requires renewable dispatcher liveness. Operator bindings and recovery
mutations have exact identity, generation, idempotency and evidence contracts in configuration.txt.

All acceptance IDs require mandatory mapped executable qualification. Deadline expiry cannot mask
deadlock in liveness models. Duplicate effect starts have zero tolerance, including explained ones.
The low-allocation dispatcher-latency benchmark remains a required implementation qualification,
not a measured result or a claim that every listed small allocation can fit the engine.


H. Review F amendments: recovery, authority and progress
------------------------------------------------------
The domain-coordination ADR explicitly resolves a collision with the Unix-native XDG value:
canonical rendezvous and default domain anchor use the account database's XDG-shaped default
<OS-account-home>/.local/state/af/resources independently of environment overrides. This narrow
exception prevents changed HOME/XDG roots from creating second budgets or temporary anchors.
Task Stores, normal configuration, telemetry and runtime sockets retain their ordinary roots.
Persistent registry coordination uses a lock file on its qualified durable filesystem; transient
pulse/IPC records use the canonical registration's qualified runtime locator and generation,
independent of caller XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/TMPDIR. Its default is an ephemeral runtime subdirectory
of canonical domain state; this shared locator is also in the explicitly scoped XDG exception.
These disposable records are not recovery authority. Arbitrary shared
network homes remain unsupported unless their identity/locking/durability contract is qualified.

The independent write-ahead Store/executor inventory makes snapshot and no-snapshot recovery
complete when evidence survives; loss of both inventory and authority requires restoration or
external fencing, never a blind reset. Predecessor lookup and epoch succession are explicit.
Native cooperative reboot retirement records local-marker/owner-asserted continuity risk; strict
release still requires qualified termination evidence. Container in-place recovery requires an
external instance supervisor; unsupported durability is refused before admission.

Operator capabilities are excluded from worker bindings; nested work receives parent scope only.
Same-user cooperative execution cannot claim adversarial human-versus-model separation. Execution
resource constraints resolve into the existing Execution Binding quota authority. Dispatcher
liveness uses separate pulse records; stopped SQLite writers have an explicit unavailable state.
Remote sealing prevents publication/selection but does not retroactively stop a selected holder.
Accumulated eligible wait pauses across inactivity and rejoin competes at the next free turn.

Qualification mappings bind exact injection/outcome clauses and required fault/assertion hooks.
Disposable allocation plus rendezvous isolation is mandatory before real-process fault injection.
Formal cost and dispatcher latency are unmeasured implementation gates, not demonstrated success.

I. Review G: current capacity, unresolved authority, and executable claims
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Boot/instance re-attribution is not proof of termination: strict permits remain unresolved while
old-local work no longer charges current kernel capacity. Retained/shared storage and remote
obligations stay charged. Registered instance-scoped containers preserve shared scheduling without
claiming externally proven restart recovery. Store active/quiescent evidence outlives path cleanup.
Nested work proxies through parent control; strict operator separation also protects domain files.
Operator bindings can replace estimates in either direction, never project-required constraints.
The domain ADR also pins the shared runtime locator. Symmetric coordinators read non-durable boot-monotonic pulses; 4250ms loss and 94s fixture
bounds include observation/selection allowances. FIFO live-slot offers preserve an admitted
waiter until admission or loss of eligibility/liveness; no periodic rotation exists.
Amend ADR-0150/0154's failure mapping with resource_exceeded(memory/pids), negative_result and
explicit simultaneous-reason precedence; preserve breach-first mutation handling and native Task budgets.
Required-case mappings distinguish runtime/production simulation from supplemental formal/Loom
proof. Benchmark targets activate only after reviewed measurements of the full declared tier.

J. Review I: slot ownership, bootstrap and bounded uncertainty
--------------------------------------------------------------
Task slots/group caps are vector coordinates. A holder's remaining work outside the finite drain
set makes its slot non-finite N; new-root protection must not deadlock existing Task progress.
FIFO live-slot offers keep a healthy admitted waiter live; periodic quantum rotation is removed.
Non-durable shared-memory pulses preserve continuity through durable-writer stalls; only scheduling
changes/checkpoints and inventory transitions sync. The fixed administrator-owned locator mapping
supports network-home and home-less/service accounts without per-invocation independent budgets.
This bootstrap is another narrowly defined Unix-native/XDG exception in the same domain ADR.
Unresolvable predecessor-local authority has a terminal inconclusive, permanently sealed disposition
with bounded Provider-slot horizon and metadata; remote executor obligations remain evidence-gated.
Closed registry predicates now express shared coordination and proven restart recovery separately.
Preserve ADR-0154 breach-first classification, including mutation secondary and explicit pids policy.
The calibration authority names source/build/runner identities, five fresh runs, statistic and
case/cell selection before a candidate campaign. No benchmark success is claimed in this proposal.

K. Review J: reserve slot opportunity and freeze empirical gates
----------------------------------------------------------------
A root's FIFO slot claim precedes resource aging; later fitting roots cannot repeatedly steal
its next slot. Claims consume slot capacity but no coordinator footprint before admission.
Active roots retain their control/progress path. One global claimant orders optional group caps.
First administrator locator entry and removal are fenced succession, with tombstones and Store
journal checks. Each admission revalidates locator identity. Benchmark defaults are activated
only against declared numeric cells, outcomes, sample counts, timing/neighbor thresholds and
independent holdout runs; expanded-policy runs do not certify the defaults.

L. Review K: every slot claim is pressure-bounded; adoption survives mapping deletion
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The single slot claimant remains unique until admission/lapse. The30s pressure grace also binds
unprotected claims; observation hysteresis prevents immediate reclaim. Fixed administrator
adoption history and local adoption tombstones make deleted mappings distinguishable from
first use. Overhead uses one same-candidate static-control baseline and exact per-rate statistics.
Observer freshness includes old sample age and a declared jitter margin. D08 and O06/QT69 have
explicit required runtime receipts. K's architecture output is partial evidence, not acceptance.

M. Review L: scope protection and make benchmark work comparable
----------------------------------------------------------------
Group-blocked roots release unused global slots and the claimant role. They retain original
queue order on overlapping groups; disjoint groups make progress without waiting for that holder.
Slot vectors are atomic, and fixed start bounds name prior-claim/turn premises. Domain-wide stale
retention is distinct from scoped stale: a fresh competitor bounds the latter by30s grace plus
qualified handoff, with preserved age and five fresh samples before reclaim. Existing live-work
charges never release merely because an unused claim or protected turn does.

Overhead and neighbor comparisons use the same-candidate static-control baseline. Exact logical
work and boundary states must match in qualifying segments; admission timestamps may differ.
Reject post-hoc cohort filtering and zero-work CPU wins. The release arithmetic includes paired
warmups and the last-accepted-build saturation ladder:426.4 of512 core-hours before remaining
setup/fault work. That subtotal is a planning constraint, not evidence of campaign feasibility.
